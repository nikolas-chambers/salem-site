Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Information We Collect

The Salem app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking SDKs, and we do not operate any server that stores information about you. Guide content is bundled with the app or loaded from public APIs. The only time information leaves your device is when you actively use a network feature — weather, maps, walking directions, or one of the AI features (chat, tarot, and the optional readings on the rune and I Ching screens) — as described below.

Location Data

The app may request location permission solely to show your position on the map. This data is processed entirely on-device and is never sent to any server. Location permission can be denied or revoked at any time without affecting other app features.

Network Requests

The app makes the following network requests:

  • National Weather Service API — for weather forecasts and current conditions (uses a fixed Salem, MA coordinate, not your device location)
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers — for map imagery; tiles are requested for the area shown on screen
  • OpenStreetMap-based routing (OSRM) — to draw walking trails between fixed points of interest; your device location is not sent
  • Google Gemini API — for the AI chat guide, tarot readings, and the optional readings on the rune-casting and I Ching screens, and for translating weather text into non-English languages (see below)
  • GitHub Pages (this site) — the User Gallery requests the current photo list and the photographs themselves from here, so opening that screen means this host sees your device's IP address, as any website you visit does
  • Google Play — if you installed from the Play Store, the app asks Play whether a newer version is available so it can offer an update

No identifying information is included in these requests beyond what is necessary for the API to function.

Astrology Charts

The astrology chart is calculated entirely on your device, from published public-domain astronomical data bundled with the app. Birth details you enter — name, date, time, place — stay on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere to draw the chart.

If you save a chart, those details are stored on your device only, in the app's own settings. Charts you save may be other people's; nothing about them leaves your phone either. Deleting a saved chart, or uninstalling the app, removes them.

The chart screen has one optional button that does send something. Asking for an AI reading transmits the calculated planetary positions — not the name, not the birthplace, not anything you typed. You should know that those positions still describe the moment of birth closely enough to pin it down. The chart, the wheel and every placement are drawn without that button ever being pressed.

Palm Reading

The palm reading screen works from a photograph of a hand, either taken with the camera or chosen from your photo library. The app asks first and explains what will happen before either option opens. The photo is then scaled down, sent to Google's Gemini API to produce the reading, and discarded.

The palm reading screen never writes the photo to storage and sends it nowhere else. We keep no copy of it, and we operate no server for one to sit on. Google processes the image under its own Privacy Policy. Photograph a hand and nothing more — whatever else is in the frame is sent along with it. The whole feature is optional, and nothing is sent unless you pick a photo yourself.

AI Features & What Is Sent

The AI tour guide, the tarot readings, and the optional readings on the rune-casting, I Ching, astrology chart and palm reading screens are powered by Google's Gemini API. Text you type into those screens is sent to Google to generate the reply, and Google processes it under its own Privacy Policy. Nothing is stored on any server of ours.

Because your typed messages leave the device when you use these features, do not enter personal or sensitive information into the chat or tarot screens. AI-generated responses may be inaccurate and are provided for entertainment and general information only.

The rune and I Ching screens are different in one respect worth stating: they take no typed input at all. If you ask for a reading, what is sent is only which runes or which hexagram the cast produced. Both screens work fully without that button — the draw and every meaning are on the device, and the AI reading is an extra rather than the feature.

Separately, when the app is set to a language other than English, the English weather text supplied by the National Weather Service is sent to the same API to be translated. Those requests contain only the public forecast wording — never your location, your messages, or anything identifying you. Translations are cached on your device so a given phrase is sent at most once.

Feedback & Suggestions

The in-app Suggestions feature opens your device's email app with your message pre-filled, addressed to the developer. It is only sent if you choose to send it, and goes directly to the developer's inbox — it is not collected or stored by us otherwise.

Photo Submissions & the User Gallery

Submitting a photo is optional and works the same way as Suggestions: the app opens your email app with the picture attached, and nothing leaves your device unless you send that message yourself. Nothing is uploaded in the background, and there is no server for it to be uploaded to.

Photos are chosen by hand, and most are never used. A photo that is chosen is published in the gallery, credited to the name you gave — or as Anonymous if you gave none. The gallery is served from this website rather than built into the app, so what you see there is whatever is currently published. Location data held in the photo file is stripped before publication.

Before sending, you are asked to confirm that the photo is yours to give and that anyone recognisable in it agreed to appear. You keep ownership of your photograph. If you change your mind, write to the address at the foot of this page and it comes down. Because the gallery is served from the web rather than shipped inside the app, that takes effect straight away — you do not have to wait for an app update.

Tips

The Tip Your Guide screen is the only place the app asks you for money, and it is entirely optional. Tips are one-time purchases processed by Google Play Billing. The payment happens inside Play's own checkout — the app never sees, handles or stores your card details, billing address or name, and receives back only whether Play reported the purchase as complete, pending or failed.

A tip unlocks nothing. There is no paid tier, no removed advertising to buy, and no screen that behaves differently afterwards; the app is identical before and after. Nothing about whether you have tipped is recorded, transmitted or remembered between sessions — the purchase is consumed as soon as Play confirms it, which is also what lets you tip again if you want to.

Refunds, receipts and payment disputes are handled by Google Play under its own terms, not by us.

What We Don't Do

  • No account creation or sign-in.
  • No advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No paywall. Nothing in the app is locked, and no purchase unlocks anything — see Tips above.
  • We never sell or licence your data to anyone. Photos submitted for the gallery are used only in the app.

Third-Party Services

Links within the app open external websites in your device browser. Those sites operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

Google Play Billing processes optional tips, as described above. Google handles the payment under its own Privacy Policy; we receive no payment details.

Children's Privacy

Salem is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
